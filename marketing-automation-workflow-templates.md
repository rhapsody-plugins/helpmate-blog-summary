## What Are Marketing Automation Workflow Templates?

Marketing automation workflow templates are pre-built sequences of automated actions triggered by customer behavior. They combine email timing, message content, and conditional logic into reusable frameworks that work without manual intervention.

Effective templates share common traits:
- Clear entry triggers
- Multiple touchpoints
- Conditional branching based on customer actions
- Exit conditions that stop the sequence when goals are met

For WooCommerce stores, templates must integrate with your commerce platform. They need to access order data, cart contents, and customer segments. Native WordPress solutions connect directly to your store without complex API configurations.

## 10 Essential Workflow Templates

### Template 1: Welcome Series for New Subscribers
Welcome emails generate 320% more revenue per email than other promotional emails (Campaign Monitor, 2025).

- **Trigger:** Email signup via popup, checkout, or account creation
- **Email 1 (Immediate):** Welcome message with brand story and 10% discount code
- **Email 2 (Day 2):** Bestsellers showcase with social proof
- **Email 3 (Day 5):** Educational content plus expiring discount reminder
- **Exit Condition:** First purchase completed

### Template 2: Abandoned Cart Recovery
Abandoned cart emails achieve 44.76% open rates and 10.7% conversion rates (Mailmend, 2026).

- **Trigger:** Cart abandoned for 1 hour (minimum $25 value)
- **Email 1 (1 hour):** Friendly reminder with cart contents and direct checkout link
- **Email 2 (24 hours):** Social proof plus free shipping offer
- **Email 3 (72 hours):** 10% discount with scarcity messaging
- **SMS (optional, 48 hours):** Short cart reminder with link

### Template 3: Post-Purchase Follow-Up Sequence
The post-purchase window is your best opportunity to generate reviews, prevent returns, and drive repeat purchases.

- **Email 1 (Immediate):** Order confirmation with tracking information
- **Email 2 (Delivery day):** Delivery confirmation with usage tips
- **Email 3 (7 days after delivery):** Review request with photo incentive
- **Email 4 (14 days):** Cross-sell complementary products
- **Email 5 (30 days):** Replenishment reminder (if applicable)

### Template 4: Win-Back Campaign for Inactive Customers
Acquiring a new customer costs 5x more than retaining an existing one (HubSpot, 2025).

- **Trigger:** No purchase in 90 days
- **Email 1 (Day 90):** "We miss you" message with 15% discount
- **Email 2 (Day 97):** Showcase new arrivals since last purchase
- **Email 3 (Day 105):** Final offer with 20% discount and urgency
- **Exit Condition:** Purchase made or unsubscribe

### Template 5: Browse Abandonment Recovery
Browse abandonment affects 92% of website visitors (SaleCycle, 2025).

- **Trigger:** Viewed 3+ product pages but no cart addition
- **Email 1 (30 minutes):** "Still thinking it over?" with viewed products
- **Email 2 (24 hours):** Social proof for viewed products plus similar items
- **Email 3 (72 hours):** Limited-time 10% discount on viewed items

### Template 6: Birthday or Anniversary Campaign
Birthday emails generate 342% higher revenue per email than standard promotional emails (Experian, 2025).

- **Trigger:** 7 days before customer birthday
- **Email 1 (7 days before):** Birthday gift announcement with 20% discount
- **Email 2 (On birthday):** Personalized birthday message with gift reminder
- **Email 3 (3 days after):** "Your gift expires soon" urgency message

### Template 7: Back-in-Stock Notification
Back-in-stock notifications convert at 22.45% on average (SaleCycle, 2025).

- **Trigger:** Product restocked (WooCommerce inventory update)
- **Email 1 (Immediate):** "It is back" notification with direct purchase link
- **SMS (optional):** Short restock alert for high-demand items
- **Follow-up (48 hours):** Stock running low warning

### Template 8: Order Status and Shipping Updates
93% of customers want proactive shipping notifications (Narvar, 2025).

- **Email 1 (Processing):** Order confirmed, preparing for shipment
- **Email 2 (Shipped):** Tracking number with carrier link
- **Email 3 (Out for delivery):** Arrival expected today
- **Email 4 (Delivered):** Delivery confirmation with satisfaction check

### Template 9: Product Review Request Sequence
Products with 50+ reviews convert 270% better than those without reviews (Spiegel Research Center, 2025).

- **Trigger:** 7 days after order delivered
- **Email 1 (Day 7):** Review request with direct link and photo upload option
- **Email 2 (Day 14):** Reminder with incentive (loyalty points or discount)
- **Email 3 (Day 21):** Final request highlighting importance of feedback

### Template 10: VIP Customer Re-engagement
VIP customers generate 60% of revenue for most e-commerce stores.

- **Trigger:** Customer with 3+ orders, no purchase in 60 days
- **Email 1 (Day 60):** Exclusive early access to new collection
- **Email 2 (Day 67):** Personal note with 25% VIP discount
- **Direct outreach (Day 74):** Personal email from customer success team

## Best Practices for Success

**Segment your audience.** New subscribers need education. Repeat buyers need appreciation. Lapsed customers need incentives.

**Personalize beyond first names.** Include product recommendations based on browsing history. Reference past purchases. Personalized emails deliver 6x higher transaction rates (Experian, 2025).

**Test and optimize continuously.** A/B test subject lines, send times, and discount levels. Even small improvements compound over thousands of sends.

**Respect unsubscribe requests immediately.** Your automation platform must handle opt-outs automatically to maintain GDPR and CAN-SPAM compliance.
